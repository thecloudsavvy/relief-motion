Home · About Us

Physiotherapy care, connected.

Relief Motion Physiotherapy is building a more connected way to access rehabilitation care in Nigeria. We connect individuals with physiotherapy services and work towards partnerships that can make quality rehabilitation more accessible across communities and healthcare settings.

From home visits and online consultations to our growing physiotherapist network, we aim to make finding and accessing professional physiotherapy simpler.

Move. Recover. Live Better.

Our mission

To make quality physiotherapy more accessible by connecting patients, qualified physiotherapists and healthcare organisations through convenient services and meaningful partnerships.

How we work

Care centred on the patient

We consider each patient’s condition, goals, location and needs when helping them access suitable physiotherapy services.

A connected physiotherapy network

We are building relationships with qualified physiotherapists to help connect patients with appropriate care based on location, availability and clinical needs.

Convenient access to care

Our home physiotherapy and online consultation services offer different ways for people to access support, depending on their circumstances and clinical suitability.

Partnerships that expand access

We welcome conversations with clinics, hospitals, HMOs, employers and community organisations interested in exploring physiotherapy services, referral arrangements and rehabilitation initiatives.

Our commitment

We believe good rehabilitation goes beyond a single appointment. It requires professional care, clear communication, appropriate follow-up and respect for every patient’s privacy.

We aim to build trust through responsible clinician verification, patient-centred service and a commitment to appropriate professional standards.

Building the future of physiotherapy

Relief Motion is growing beyond a home-visit service towards a connected physiotherapy model that brings together patients, practitioners and healthcare organisations.

We are building this step by step, starting with the services we can provide today and developing partnerships that can help us reach more people over time.
