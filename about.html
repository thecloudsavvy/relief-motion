Physiotherapy care, connected.

Relief Motion connects people and healthcare organisations with professional physiotherapy. Patients book home or online care. Clinics and hospitals can ask about a partnership. Move. Recover. Live Better.

How we work

You never get a random phone number. Relief Motion stays your point of contact from the first message to follow-up. Every physiotherapist is checked against the MRTB register before they see a patient.

Our own patient charts sit in a private staff system with role-based access and an audit trail. We will not claim hospital or HMO programmes that are not live.
