For clinic managers, hospital administrators and clinical leads. Tell us what you need. We will say clearly what we can and cannot do today.

What is live

Relief Motion already delivers home physiotherapy and online / tele-rehabilitation across Nigeria, with MRTB-verified clinicians matched to the patient.

What we can talk about

If your facility needs extra physiotherapy capacity, post-discharge home follow-up, or a small pilot, we can start with a discovery conversation. We do not currently offer inpatient programmes, hospital staffing contracts, or HMO coverage as live products.

How a conversation works

  • You send an enquiry to info@reliefmotionphysio.com
  • We hold a discovery call
  • We map what you need against what we can actually deliver
  • If it fits, we agree a limited pilot and review it together

Records and governance

Patient records for our own caseload sit in a private staff system with role-based access and an audit trail. For a clinic partnership, we can set up a clinic login and dashboard so the facility can follow those patients’ records and progress.
