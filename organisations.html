Home · HMO and organisations

Partnership opportunities, not live contracts.

Relief Motion is open to discussions with HMOs, employers, private hospitals and community organisations. Nothing on this page is an established service or a signed programme unless we agree it in writing.

Our current scope

Home visits and online consultations are available now. Hospital staffing, inpatient programmes and HMO coverage are not live services. They are partnership conversations, not products you can buy today.

Who this is for

  • Health Maintenance Organisations (HMOs)
  • Employers and corporate health programmes
  • Private hospitals and clinics
  • Community organisations

What we could offer

Depending on location, demand and a written scope, a partnership conversation might cover:

  • Home and online physiotherapy for referred patients
  • Rehabilitation support after discharge
  • Matching MRTB-verified physiotherapists in cities we can cover
  • A limited pilot, reviewed before anything is extended

Where we can work today

Home visits and online consultations are available across Nigeria. We match an MRTB-verified physiotherapist in the patient’s city, or online if that fits better. Coverage for a named HMO or employer panel is not automatic and is not live until we agree it.

How a discussion starts

  • Write to info@reliefmotionphysio.com
  • Tell us the organisation, locations and what you need
  • We map that against what we can actually deliver
  • If it fits, we agree a limited pilot and review it together
