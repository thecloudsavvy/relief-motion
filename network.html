Home · Network

Join the physiotherapist network.

We match patients across Nigeria with MRTB-verified physiotherapists for home visits and online sessions. Expressing interest is not a job offer and not a guarantee of assignments.

Who this is for

Licensed physiotherapists who can deliver care in their city, or online, and who want to be considered when a suitable patient is booked.

What we check

  • MRTB registration
  • Name, city and the kind of conditions you treat
  • That you can actually cover the locations you list

How assignments work

When a patient books, Relief Motion remains the point of contact. We only share what is needed for that visit. Work depends on demand, your location and fit, not on signing up.
