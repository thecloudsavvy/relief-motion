How it works

You never get a random phone number. Relief Motion stays your point of contact from the first message to the follow-up.

  1. Tell us what is going on

    Message WhatsApp with the condition, your city, and a preferred time. Home visits and online sessions are available nationwide.

  2. We match a physiotherapist

    We assign an MRTB-verified clinician who covers your area and treats your condition.

  3. Treatment at home or online

    Your physiotherapist comes to you, or joins you on a video call, on the time you agreed.

  4. We stay with you

    After the session we check in, collect feedback, and book the next visit if you need one.
