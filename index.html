Patients & caregivers

Book care

  1. Message WhatsApp

    Tell us the condition, your city and a preferred time.

  2. We match a physiotherapist

    An MRTB-verified clinician who covers your area, or online if that fits better.

  3. Treatment at home or on video

    Your physiotherapist comes to you, or joins you on a call, at the time you agreed.

  4. We stay with you

    We check in after the session and book the next visit if you need one.

Clinics & hospitals

Discuss a partnership

  1. Send an enquiry

    Email info@reliefmotionphysio.com with the organisation, location and what you need.

  2. Discovery conversation

    We listen first. No hospital staffing, inpatient or HMO cover is offered as a live product today.

  3. Scope, if it fits

    If we can help, we agree a limited pilot, not a nationwide contract by default.

  4. Review

    We review together before anything is extended.

Why Relief Motion

  • Verified against the MRTB register

    Every physiotherapist is verified before they see a patient.

  • Personalised care

    Matched to your condition, goals and care needs, not a one-size-fits-all plan.

  • From first booking to follow-up

    We stay involved so care stays organised and consistent.

  • Care around real life

    Home visits and online consultations make physiotherapy easier to keep.

  • A network we are still building
