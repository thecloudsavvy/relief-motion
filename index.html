A more connected approach to physiotherapy

Relief Motion brings together professional care, convenient access and a growing network to help make rehabilitation easier to find and access.

  • Patient-centred care

    We help individuals access physiotherapy suited to their conditions, goals and circumstances.

  • Care beyond the clinic

    Home visits and online consultations offer flexible ways to access physiotherapy where appropriate.

  • A growing physiotherapist network

    We are building connections with qualified physiotherapists to expand access to care across Nigeria.

  • Clinical and healthcare partnerships

    We welcome opportunities to collaborate with clinics, hospitals and other healthcare providers to explore practical rehabilitation services.

  • Community and corporate outreach

    We aim to support initiatives that bring physiotherapy education, prevention and rehabilitation closer to the communities and organisations that need them.

  • Quality, trust and continuity

    We prioritise professional standards, appropriate clinician verification, patient privacy and coordinated care.
