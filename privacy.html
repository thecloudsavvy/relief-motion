Privacy Policy

How Relief Motion uses the information you share with us. This is not a published NDPR certification.

Website and enquiries

If you email info@reliefmotionphysio.com or use a form on this site, we use the details you send to reply and to discuss care or a partnership. We do not sell that information.

Bookings on WhatsApp

Patient bookings are made on WhatsApp. WhatsApp is a third-party service with its own privacy terms. Share only what is needed to match a physiotherapist: condition, city and a preferred time.

Clinical records

Records for patients in our own caseload sit in a private staff system with role-based access and an audit trail. We do not publish patient names, charts or clinical images on this website.

Requests

To ask about information we hold, write to info@reliefmotionphysio.com.
